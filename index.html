FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything customers usually ask before ordering.

How much does an advertisement cost?

Video advertisements start at E150 for 15 seconds and rise to E2,000 for 5 minutes. Audio advertisements start at E100 for 15 seconds and rise to E1,000 for 5 minutes. Image advertisements start from E50 and text advertisements from E20. All prices are starting rates, and the final quotation is confirmed after we review your brief.

How long does production take?

Text and image advertisements are usually ready within 2 to 3 working days. Video and audio advertisements typically take 3 to 7 working days depending on length and complexity. Your exact production time is confirmed with your quotation.

How do I pay?

Once you confirm your order you receive an order number and your request is marked as pending payment. AD contacts you on your preferred channel to confirm the quotation and arrange payment before production begins.

Can I request changes after I see the draft?

Yes. Every package includes revisions as agreed in your quotation. You review a preview, request the included revisions, and the final advertisement is delivered once you approve it.

How do I send my logo, images or script?

You can upload files directly in the order form. If your files are large, submit your order first, then send the files to AD on WhatsApp and quote your order number so we can match everything up.

Do you write the script or copy for me?

Yes. Scriptwriting for video and audio, and copywriting for text advertisements, are part of the service. Simply describe what you want to promote in your brief, or send your own script if you already have one.

What happens after I press Confirm & Pay?

Your order is saved and you receive an order number such as AD20261101-1530K7Q. Your request is marked pending payment while AD reviews it and reaches out on your preferred contact option to confirm everything and arrange payment.

Do you produce advertisements for social media?

Yes. Video and image advertisements are produced in the sizes you need for Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp status and other platforms. Mention the platforms in your brief.
